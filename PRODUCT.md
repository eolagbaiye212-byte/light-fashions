# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Young believers and streetwear buyers (roughly 17–30), mostly on phones, most often arriving from @children_ofthelight on Instagram right after a drop post, a runway recap, or a "1 of 1 available now" story. They already like the clothes. They want to see the piece on a body, pick a size, and check out before a one-of-one is gone. A second audience is press, stylists and collaborators arriving after the NYFW show, who need to understand what LIGHT is in under a minute.

## Product Purpose
The official store for LIGHT (Children of the Light), the faith-led streetwear label designed by Myron (@divine.myron). It replaces a stock Shopify Dawn theme with a storefront that feels like the brand: the Light Fashion Experience runway (NYFW, Sept 13 2026), scripture-led looks, and limited one-of-one garments. It runs headless on the existing Shopify store: live products, prices and stock come from Shopify, and the bag hands off to the real Shopify checkout. Success: more drop-day conversions from Instagram traffic, sold-out one-of-ones, and a site people screenshot and share.

## Brand Personality
Devout, radiant, streetwise. Confident without preaching; the scripture is the brand's own voice, not decoration. Copy is short and plain, in the founder's real words where possible ("Light is what I call a God Vision."). Emotional goal: walking out of a dark room into flash light — awe first, then desire, then ease.

## Anti-references
- The current Dawn theme: centered logo, generic product grids, "View all" buttons, nothing about the show.
- Generic Christian merch stores: stock crosses, clip-art doves, pastel gradients, Bible-verse-of-the-day widgets.
- Hypebeast template stores: all-caps everything, countdown timers, fake scarcity banners.
- Luxury minimalism that hides the product (tiny grey type, endless whitespace, no prices).
- Anything that reads as AI-generated: identical card grids, eyebrow labels over every heading, gradient text, glass cards.

## Design Principles
1. **The photographs are the set.** The runway images were shot on a dark slatted wall with amber light rods; the site extends that set instead of framing photos in boxes.
2. **From darkness into light.** The site moves from the night of the runway into the white light of the store (Ephesians 5:8). The transition is the one signature moment; everything else stays disciplined.
3. **Scripture is content, not ornament.** Each look carries the verse the designer chose for it. Verses are quoted exactly and cited.
4. **Scarcity is real, so say it plainly.** One-of-ones, pre-releases and sold-out sizes are stated as facts from live stock, never manufactured urgency.
5. **Two taps to the bag.** From any look or product, size and add-to-bag are always one step away; checkout is Shopify's own.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on both the night and day surfaces. Full keyboard support for the bag drawer, size picker and runway carousel (arrow keys, visible focus). `prefers-reduced-motion` replaces the light transition, video autoplay and scroll-linked motion with static frames and crossfades. Videos are muted by default, never auto-play sound, and have pause controls. Alt text describes the garment and the look, not "image of model".
