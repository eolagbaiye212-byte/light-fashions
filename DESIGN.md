# Design

## Concept
**Darkness into light.** Ephesians 5:8, the verse the brand is named after: "For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light." The site has two surfaces and one gesture that connects them.

- **Night** is the runway set: the dark slatted wall from the Light Fashion Experience shoot, sampled directly from the photographs so images bleed into the page instead of sitting in frames.
- **Day** is the store: flash white, where product packshots live natively.
- **The rod** is the amber light bar visible behind the models. It is the only accent and the only signature motion: it opens the hero video on load, and it widens into white light when you scroll from the runway into the shop.

Scene sentence: a 22-year-old in a dim room at night, phone in hand, who just watched the NYFW recap on Instagram and wants the jacket before the one-of-one is gone.

## Typography
The type is the calm counterpart to the chrome mark and the runway photographs: a clean, grown-up retail grotesque in sentence case, with hierarchy carried by size and space rather than caps or heaviness. References for character (not templates): UNIQLO and boohoo.

- **Hanken Grotesk** (variable, `wght` 100–900, OFL, `app/fonts/hanken-grotesk-variable.woff2`, Latin + Latin Extended). Sets everything: headings, UI, body, prices. Normal proportions, figures are tabular by default so prices and counts align. Fallback: a metric-matched Arial generated by `next/font`, then Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / system UI.
- **Frank Ruhl Libre** (OFL, Latin + Hebrew). Kept for one restrained role: quoted words, scripture and Myron's, via the `quote-*` steps and `Verse`. It's a Hebrew-heritage text serif (the face of Hebrew Bibles), and the brand already speaks in Hebrew (I Love Jesus Hebrew Tee, "YAH", "Yeshua", אור on the story page). Never used for headings or UI.
- Weights: 400 reading text; 500 headings, nav, chips, product names, disclosure titles; 600 prices, buttons, form legends and stock facts (1 of 1, sold out). 300 only for the large look numbers.
- Body 17px / 1.55 on day, 1.62 on night. Lead copy is fluid (17→20px). UI 15px, fine print 13px. Measure ≤ 68ch.
- No uppercase headings, no tracked-out eyebrows. Headings and UI labels are sentence case; "Light Fashion Experience" and product names keep their own capitals.
- Headings use these steps only (utilities in `globals.css`); never an ad-hoc `text-[clamp()]` on a heading or quote:

| Step | Use | Size | Weight / leading / tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| `type-hero` | the two runway heroes | 42→72px | 500 / 1.02 / −0.03em |
| `type-h1` | page titles, menu | 36→60px | 500 / 1.04 / −0.028em |
| `type-h2` | section titles, product name | 28→44px | 500 / 1.08 / −0.022em |
| `type-h3` | sub-sections, story titles, info headings | 22→30px | 500 / 1.16 / −0.016em |
| `type-h4` | small headings, timeline dates | 18→22px | 500 / 1.28 / −0.01em |
| `type-num` | look numbers 01–16 | 44→72px | 300 / 0.90 / −0.035em |
| `quote-xl` | the Ephesians verse, founder quote, runway close | 30→52px | serif 400 / 1.14 |
| `quote-lg` | a look's verse on runway and product pages, footer verse | 24→36px | serif 400 / 1.22 |
| `quote-md` | verses in the shop's look bands | 21→28px | serif 400 / 1.28 |
| `quote-sm` | verses in the home rail | 19px | serif 400 / 1.38 |

  Larger steps tighten their tracking and leading; smaller steps relax them. Headings balance their line breaks; long quotes use `pretty`.
- Every section opens with `SectionHead` (title, short muted intro, optional action on the right).

## Layout
- 12-column grid, 16px side gutter on mobile, 24–40px on desktop, content max 1440px; imagery goes full-bleed.
- Spacing comes from one fluid scale (4px base): `--space-lg` heading → content (32→48px), `--space-xl` between blocks (48→72px), `--space-band` stacked related bands such as stories and looks (56→88px, divided by a hairline), `--space-section` a standalone section (72→128px). Utilities: `section-y`, `band-y`, `page-top` (clears the fixed header), `head-gap`, `block-gap`.
- Content that sits under the fixed header is always in normal flow with `page-top` (or equivalent) clearance, never absolutely positioned against it.
- Transitions between sections are part of the rhythm, not leftovers:
  - The section after the light transition rises into its white landing (`.after-light`). The pull-up is measured off the verse (`--quote-xl-size`), so the verse and "From the show" sit one `--space-section` apart at any screen height. It's off for reduced motion.
  - A hairline divides two sections on the same surface; where night meets day the change of surface is the divider, so no line is drawn.
  - Blocks inside one section (the story intro and the founder quote, for example) are separated by `--space-xl`, not a section gap.
- Pairs of image and text alternate sides down a page (story bands, then the founder quote with the poster on the left). Lists with a title, such as the 2026 timeline, put the title in four columns and the list in eight on desktop, so each line sits near its picture.
- Look numbers 01–16 are the real running order of the show, so they are numbered. Nothing else is.
- No cards with borders or shadows. Products sit on a flat tile; type sits under the image.

## Brand mark
- Chrome wordmark on night. On day surfaces, the gunmetal cut of the same mark (`public/brand/light-chrome-day.webp`, derived from the chrome by tone mapping; 9.6:1 against white vs 2.7:1 for the chrome). The header crossfades between them as it changes surface.
- The header and menu use pre-sized cuts at 3× the header height (`light-chrome-mark.webp`, `light-chrome-day-mark.webp`, 271×96) served `unoptimized`, so the mark never waits on the image optimizer. The footer's full-width marks go through `next/image`.

## Motion
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (expo-out) for reveals, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (quint-out) for UI. Exits run faster than entrances.
- Durations: 160ms micro, 280–460ms drawers, 160–440ms page transitions, 900–1400ms the hero opening.
- Feedback lives on the control that was used (the add button becomes "Added"), not in toasts.

### System (all compositor-friendly: transform, opacity, clip-path)
| Layer | How | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Smooth wheel scroll | Lenis (`components/SmoothScroll.tsx`), mouse/trackpad only, paused while any dialog is open | everywhere |
| The light | CSS scroll-driven timeline (`.light-*`), rAF fallback writes `--beam`/`--white` | home |
| Page transitions | React `<ViewTransition>` via `components/PageTransition.tsx`; lateral = crossfade + 8px lift, `nav-forward`/`nav-back` = 24px slide | every page |
| Product morph | named `product-{id}` view transition from tile photo to the product gallery's first photo | tiles → product page |
| Filter reflow | per-tile `<ViewTransition>` + `startTransition(router.replace)`; chips use `useOptimistic` | /shop |
| Scroll-linked | `.drift` on large editorial photographs only (shop look bands, a story's lead frame, the story artwork, the product page's runway shot): ±2.5%, `animation-timeline: view()`. Product grids, lists, runway look frames and headings stay still. | bands, stories |
| Image arrival | `FadeImage` fades Shopify images in on load | tiles, galleries, bag |
| Header | hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up; publishes `--header-offset` for sticky bars | global |

- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no autoplay video, no hero opening, static light, no scroll-linked motion, no smooth scroll, instant view transitions.
- Never animate on more than one axis of attention at once: the rod moments are the only page-level choreography.

## Components
Header (text links, theme follows the surface underneath), Bag drawer (native `<dialog>`), Size picker (radio group, sold-out sizes struck and disabled; adding without a size shows one error beside the sizes, announced as an alert, and every attempt scrolls back and focuses the first available size), Shop filters (below lg the categories are one swipeable row that bleeds to the screen edge; the edge with more chips behind it fades and carries a button that pages the row, and the selected chip is kept in view; from lg up they wrap. Filters and sort live in the URL), Runway rail (scroll-snap, arrow keys, buttons), Light transition (sticky, scroll-progress driven), Product tile (image swap to runway shot on hover, quick-add sizes on hover/focus; name 500, price 600; on phones the price drops under the name so names get the tile's full width), Verse (serif quote + citation, for scripture and Myron's words alike).

Control outlines (chips, size buttons, the quantity stepper) use `--color-day-edge` (oklch 0.84), a step darker than the `--color-day-line` hairlines, so controls read as controls on white.
